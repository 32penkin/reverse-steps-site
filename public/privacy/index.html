Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Reverse Steps is a teardown journal: your photos, voice notes and notes stay on your device. This policy explains the few things that do leave it. No ads, no tracking across apps or websites, no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not sell personal data.

Who we are

Reverse Steps is developed by Yauhen Penkin (Poland), who is the data controller. Contact: penkin.yauhen@gmail.com.

Stays on your device

Projects, step photos, parts photos, voice notes, text notes, project names and goals are stored only in the app’s private storage on your phone. We do not receive them. They leave the device only when you export a project or save photos to your gallery yourself, or, if you turn them on, for reassembly hints (below). Deleting the app deletes them.

Anonymous usage statistics

To see whether the app is useful, it sends a small set of events, for example “project created”, “step captured”, “reassembly started” or “export finished”. Events contain only numbers, yes/no values and fixed options (such as the number of steps or how long saving a step took), plus a random installation ID, a random session ID, the platform (iOS or Android), app version and interface language.

Events never contain photos, audio, notes, project names, file paths, your email or account ID, or a device model. They are stored in our own database at Supabase. Because the app requires sign-in, the same random installation ID is also kept with your account in the free-teardown records (see “Account and free teardowns”), so events from that installation can technically be linked to your account. We use events only to see how the app is used, never to track you or for advertising. Raw events are deleted after 180 days; after that only totals remain. Statistics are on by default; you can turn them off any time in Settings, and then nothing is sent. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Feedback you send

If you send feedback in the app, we store the text you wrote together with the random installation ID, app version, platform and language, to read it and fix problems. If you write by email, we receive your email address and message. Please do not include personal data you do not want us to have.

Account and free teardowns

An account is required to use the app. After the introduction screens, you sign in for free with email, Google or Apple; your first sign-in creates the account. Each account gets 5 free teardowns. To count them, the app sends a random installation ID, a random project ID and your account ID to our server when you start a new teardown. Email sign-in uses a one-time code and link, without a password; the sign-in emails are sent through Gmail SMTP (Google). With Google or Apple sign-in, the provider confirms your identity; we do not receive your Google or Apple password.

We store only: your email address (with Apple it may be a private relay address) and the sign-in provider’s user ID, used only to run your account and count your free teardowns; and, for each project you start, a random project ID and a random installation ID, plus your account ID — never the project name, photos, notes or audio. If you sign in with Apple, we keep Apple’s refresh token only to revoke it when you delete the account. This data is stored at Supabase, which processes it on our behalf (data processor). Legal basis: providing the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

Reassembly hints (optional)

Hints are off until you turn them on and agree on a consent screen that names the AI provider. With your consent, when hints are written for a project, the app sends reduced copies (about 1024 px, without location or other EXIF data) of that project’s step and parts photos and your text and voice notes — voice notes as audio if the provider accepts audio, otherwise only the text notes — through our server function at Supabase to the AI provider named on the consent screen in the app, only to write the hints. The project name, the “before” cover photo and your account are not sent. The request to our server also carries the random installation ID (see below); our server calls the provider, and the provider receives only the photos and notes, not the installation ID.

Our server does not save the photos, audio or notes and does not log them. We keep only technical accounting — the random installation ID, the number of requests and photos, tokens, cost, response time and status — to apply a limit of 5 requests per day and to control costs; it is kept for up to 400 days. The provider processes the data under its paid API terms only to return the hints and does not use it to train models; it may keep it for a short period for abuse monitoring. The provider may process data outside the EU (for example in the United States) under standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision. The hints are stored on your phone only. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time by turning hints off in Settings, and you can also turn them off for a single project.

Permissions

  • Camera — to take step photos. Photos stay on the device.
  • Microphone — only when you record a voice note. Audio stays on the device.
  • Photos (add only) — only when you save a project to your gallery.

No location, no contacts, no advertising ID, no App Tracking Transparency prompt — we do not track.

Service providers

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and server functions; processes data on our behalf (data processor).
  • Google — sends the sign-in emails (Gmail SMTP) and, only if you choose it, Sign in with Google.
  • Apple — only if you choose Sign in with Apple.
  • The app stores that deliver the app.
  • The AI provider named in the app — only for reassembly hints you turned on.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website (it does not set cookies for us; GitHub may log visits for security).

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept while the account exists. Raw usage events: 180 days. Technical accounting for reassembly hints: up to 400 days. Feedback: as long as needed to handle it, at most 2 years. You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Account → Delete account) or by email. Deleting the account removes the account, your email address, the sign-in provider ID and the stored Apple token, after access is revoked with Apple. The link between your account and the teardowns you started is removed; an anonymous count of teardowns started on the device (the random installation ID and random project IDs) is kept, so the free limit cannot be reset by creating a new account. Anonymous statistics are not deleted with the account; they contain no account ID, and once the account link is removed they can no longer be connected to the account through the free-teardown records. Backups at our providers may keep data for a limited time.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction of or objection to processing, and data portability, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Write to penkin.yauhen@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), or the authority in your country.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page before any change in what the app collects (for example, naming a new AI provider) and change the date at the top.

Reverse Steps keeps a record of your own steps. It is not a repair manual and does not check that a repair is safe or correct.
