Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Reverse Steps is a teardown journal: your photos, voice notes and notes stay on your device. This policy explains the few things that do leave it. No ads, no tracking across apps or websites, no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not sell personal data.

Who we are

Reverse Steps is developed by Yauhen Penkin (Poland), who is the data controller. Contact: penkin.yauhen@gmail.com.

Stays on your device

Projects, step photos, parts photos, voice notes, text notes, project names and goals are stored only in the app’s private storage on your phone. We do not receive them. They leave the device only when you export a project or save photos to your gallery yourself, or, if you turn them on, for reassembly hints (below). Deleting the app deletes them.

Anonymous usage statistics

To see whether the app is useful, it sends a small set of events, for example “project created”, “step captured”, “reassembly started” or “export finished”. Events contain only numbers, yes/no values and fixed options (such as the number of steps or how long saving a step took), plus a random installation ID, a random session ID, the platform (iOS or Android), app version and interface language.

Events never contain photos, audio, notes, project names, file paths, your email or account ID, or a device model. They are stored in our own database at Supabase and are not linked to your account. Raw events are deleted after 180 days; after that only totals remain. Statistics are on by default; you can turn them off any time in Settings, and then nothing is sent. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Feedback you send

If you send feedback in the app, we store the text you wrote together with the random installation ID, app version, platform and language, to read it and fix problems. If you write by email, we receive your email address and message. Please do not include personal data you do not want us to have.

Account and free teardowns

Your first teardown works without an account. To count it, the app sends the random installation ID to our server when you start a new teardown. After that, a free sign-in is needed for more free teardowns. With email sign-in we process your email address; with Google or Apple sign-in, the provider confirms your identity and shares your account ID and email (Apple may give a private relay address). We do not receive your Google or Apple password.

The account stores only: account ID, email or provider ID, and the number of teardowns started (with random project references). No photos, names or notes. If you sign in with Apple, our server keeps the Apple refresh token only to revoke access when you delete the account. Legal basis: providing the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

Reassembly hints (optional)

Hints are off until you agree on a consent screen that names the AI provider. With your consent, when you ask for hints the app sends reduced copies (about 1024 px) of the step and parts photos of that project and your notes — voice notes as audio if the provider accepts it, otherwise as text transcribed on your phone — through our server function at Supabase to the AI provider named on the consent screen in the app. The cover photo is not sent.

Our server does not save the photos, audio or notes and does not log them. We keep only technical counters (number of photos, tokens, cost, response time, status) linked to the random installation ID, to apply a limit of 5 requests per day and to control costs. The provider processes the data under its API terms only to return the hints. We use only providers whose API terms do not allow training models on this data; the provider may keep it for a short period for abuse monitoring. The provider may process data outside the EU (for example in the United States) under standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision. The hints are stored on your phone only. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw in Settings at any time.

Permissions

  • Camera — to take step photos. Photos stay on the device.
  • Microphone — only when you record a voice note. Audio stays on the device.
  • Photos (add only) — only when you save a project to your gallery.

No location, no contacts, no advertising ID, no App Tracking Transparency prompt — we do not track.

Service providers

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and server functions.
  • Google and Apple — only if you choose to sign in with them, and the app stores that deliver the app.
  • The AI provider named in the app — only for reassembly hints you turned on.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website (it does not set cookies for us; GitHub may log visits for security).

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept while the account exists. Raw usage events: 180 days. Feedback: as long as needed to handle it, at most 2 years. You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account) or by email. Deleting the account removes the account, its sign-in links, the teardown counter and stored Apple tokens. Anonymous statistics are not linked to the account, so they are not affected. Backups at our providers may keep data for a limited time.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction of or objection to processing, and data portability, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Write to penkin.yauhen@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO), or the authority in your country.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page before any change in what the app collects (for example, naming a new AI provider) and change the date at the top.

Reverse Steps keeps a record of your own steps. It is not a repair manual and does not check that a repair is safe or correct.
