Account deletion

Delete your account

You can delete your account in the app, or by email if you no longer have the app or cannot sign in.

Email a deletion request

In the app

  1. Open Reverse Steps and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email, without the app

  1. Write to penkin.yauhen@gmail.com from the email address of your account (the button above prepares the message).
  2. If you signed in with Apple and hid your email, tell us that — we will find the account with you.
  3. We confirm that the account is yours by email and delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • The account and its sign-in (email, Google or Apple link).
  • The free teardown counter and project references.
  • Stored Sign in with Apple tokens, after access is revoked with Apple.

What is not affected

Projects and photos on your phone — they were never on our server; delete them in the app or by deleting the app. Anonymous statistics — they are not linked to the account and are deleted on schedule. A record that the deletion was requested and done (email and dates) is kept as proof. Provider backups may keep data for a limited time.

Reverse Steps keeps a record of your own steps. It is not a repair manual and does not check that a repair is safe or correct.
