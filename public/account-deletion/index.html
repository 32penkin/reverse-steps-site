Account deletion

Delete your account

Your account stores only your email address and the ID from your sign-in provider (email, Google or Apple). Photos, notes and project names are never part of it. You can delete the account in the app, or by email if you no longer have the app or cannot sign in.

Email a deletion request

In the app

  1. Open Reverse Steps and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. If you signed in with Apple, Apple may ask you to confirm once more.

The account is deleted immediately, and we revoke Sign in with Apple access.

By email, without the app

  1. Write to penkin.yauhen@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject “Delete my account” (the button above prepares the message).
  2. If you signed in with Apple and hid your email, tell us that — we will find the account with you.
  3. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account.
  • Your email address.
  • The user ID from your sign-in provider (email, Google or Apple).
  • The stored Sign in with Apple token, after access is revoked with Apple.
  • The link between your account and the teardowns you started.

What stays

  • An anonymous count of teardowns started on your device — a random installation ID and random project IDs, no longer linked to your account — so the free limit cannot be reset by creating a new account.
  • Anonymous usage statistics — they contain the random installation ID but not your account, and are kept for 180 days.
  • Your projects and photos — they were never on our server and stay on your phone until you delete them in the app or uninstall it.

Provider backups may keep data for a limited time.

Reverse Steps keeps a record of your own steps. It is not a repair manual and does not check that a repair is safe or correct.
