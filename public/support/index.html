Support and FAQ

Can’t find the answer? Write to penkin.yauhen@gmail.com — in English, Polish or Russian. In the app you can also use Settings → Send feedback.

Where are my photos stored?

Only on your phone, inside the app’s private storage. They are not uploaded to our server or any cloud. The only exceptions are an export you start yourself and reassembly hints if you turn them on (see below).

How do I export a project?

Open the project → Export. You can save a ZIP with all photos and notes in step order (open it on a computer or keep it in your files), or save the photos to an album in your gallery.

What happens if I delete the app?

All projects are deleted with it, because they exist only on the phone. Export the projects you want to keep first. There is no sync, so a new phone also starts empty — move projects with export.

What happens if I delete my account?

Your account, email address and sign-in provider ID are deleted on our server. An anonymous count of teardowns started on your device stays, with the link to your account removed, so a new account does not reset the free limit. Projects on the phone stay where they are. See Delete account.

How many free teardowns do I get?

Right now: 5 per account. A free sign-in is needed to use the app, and your first sign-in creates the account. You can sign in with email (a one-time code and link, no password), Google or Apple. The check happens only when you start a new teardown — a project you have started is never locked, including reassembly, export and hints. The numbers may change during the beta.

Why do I need to sign in at all?

Sign-in is required to use the app, and the account is used only to keep count of free teardowns. The account stores your email address (with Apple it may be a private relay address) and the sign-in provider’s user ID. Started teardowns are counted with a random project ID, a random installation ID and your account — no photos, project names, notes or audio.

What are reassembly hints and what is sent?

An optional feature that stays off until you turn it on. Before the first use the app asks for your consent and names the AI provider. Then reduced copies of your step and parts photos (without location data) and your text and voice notes are sent through our server to the AI provider named on the consent screen in the app, only to write a short caption for each step. We do not store them, and the provider does not use them to train models. The project name, the “before” cover photo and your account are not sent. Our server also receives your random installation ID to apply the daily limit and account for costs; it is not passed to the AI provider. Up to 5 hint requests per day. You can turn hints off in Settings or for a single project. Without consent or without internet, reassembly works from your photos as usual.

Is it safe to repair things with this app?

The app records your own steps; it does not judge whether a repair is safe. Always follow the manufacturer’s instructions, disconnect power, and do not open batteries, gas appliances or high-voltage equipment.

How do I turn off anonymous statistics?

Settings → Anonymous statistics. When it is off, nothing is sent. What is collected is listed in the privacy policy.
